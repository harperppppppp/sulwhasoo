Projects 6

A Moon Jar Shaped by Needle and Thread

We spent a special time exploring the aesthetics of traditional Korean jogakbo (patchwork) with fiber artist Ye-jin Shin (Artbo)—who reinterprets the sensibilities of traditional fabrics through modern patchwork and art objects—and crafting our own unique mosi (ramie) moon jar vase, stitch by painstaking stitch.

We also enjoyed a meaningful session reflecting on the value of tradition through a lecture on jogakbo art by Moon Ji-yoon, CEO of Bureau de Claudia, discovering the Korean aesthetics and philosophy of life embodied in a single piece of fabric.

Projects 5

Reflections with Traditional Korean Tree Art

Together with artist Woonf, who offers a modern reinterpretation of the aesthetics of traditional Korean gardens, we spent a special time wrapping up the year—stepping away from the hustle of daily life to tend to roots and prune branches, much like our ancestors did while enjoying bonsai and sharing in contemplation.

Projects 4

Seal a Story, Stroke Fortune

We spent a special time with artist “Stonepicker” Kim Ji-sun (Imalgeulsaemi)—who offers a modern interpretation of traditional seal-carving techniques—hand-carving designs into natural stone, thereby adding value and imprinting our personal tastes.

Projects 3

Sulwhasoo and Contemporary Ceramics

We experienced the beauty of ceramic art—tracing a journey from traditional to contemporary styles—and enjoyed a special session where we created our own unique patterns using the traditional yeonlimun (marbled clay) technique, guided by artist Kim Ho-jeong.

Projects 2

Wrap, the Meaning of Blessing

Through a delicate packaging style that embodies Sulwhasoo’s unique elegance—utilizing “Jihambo,” a concept combining a box (symbolizing wisdom) and a bojagi (traditional wrapping cloth)—

we explored the true meaning of packaging as a way to convey not only aesthetic beauty but also heartfelt sincerity and care,

and we took the time to experience and learn about traditional Korean wrapping methods that embody blessings and positive energy.
